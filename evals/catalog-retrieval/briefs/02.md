This is the proof moment. We have been talking about what the treatment does to a
photograph, and now the viewer has to actually see the difference rather than take
our word for it. Keep it calm and keep it honest: one picture, the untouched
version and the treated version visible together, so the eye can compare them
without cutting away or remembering a previous shot.

No narration over this beat. No text. The comparison is the whole argument, and if
the viewer has to hold two separate shots in memory to make it, we have failed.
