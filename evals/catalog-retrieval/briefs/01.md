The release goes out in five steps, and we want the viewer to hold all five in
their head at once before we drop them into the one that broke. So: lay the whole
run out across the frame, let it read as a complete route from first step to last,
then take everything away except the step where it stopped. The narrowing is the
point. By the end there should be no doubt which step we are now talking about,
and no leftover clutter from the other four.

Nothing here is being typed, spoken, or photographed. It is a status picture.
