One line on screen, and the promise inside it keeps upgrading. The sentence frame
stays put, the reader keeps reading the same sentence, but the claim in the middle
of it turns over a few times and then stops on the one we actually mean. Think of
it as the same sentence being said three ways, with the last way being the real
one.

Critical: the line must not jump around while this happens. If the words on either
side of the changing part shift position, the effect reads as broken layout rather
than as a deliberate device. It should land settled, not mid-motion.
