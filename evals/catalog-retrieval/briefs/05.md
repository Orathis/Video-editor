Two things are stacked in space: the thing the viewer is looking at now, up close,
and the thing we want them looking at instead, sitting further back. The beat is
the handoff. Attention starts on the near one and ends on the far one, and it
should feel like the eye refocusing rather than like a slide swapping out.

It happens once and it does not come back. Sharpness, weight, and a little
positional drift should all agree about where the viewer is supposed to be looking
by the end. Short beat, so the handoff cannot dawdle.
